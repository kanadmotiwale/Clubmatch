How to Use ClubMatch

Everything you need to find your perfect club in 4 simple steps

STEP 1
👤

Create Your Profile

Head to the Members page and click Create My Profile to set up your student profile with your major, year, and interests.

STEP 2
🔍

Browse Clubs

Use the search bar or category filters to find clubs that match your interests and weekly availability.

STEP 3
📋

Read Experiences

Visit the Membership Logs page to read honest reviews from real students and see average weekly hours per club.

STEP 4
🏛

Join a Club

Click on any club card to see full details and current members. Hit Join This Club to register and become a member.
